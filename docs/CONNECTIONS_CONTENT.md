# Word Connections content

Eight existing Classic boards retain their exact groups, IDs, save indices, legacy modes and content versions. Four original Challenging boards (indices 8–11) introduce compound words, phrases, homophones and alternate meanings. These are provisional chosen game tiers, not cognitive measures. English vocabulary and pronunciation may affect familiarity; player feedback and independent language/ambiguity review are pending.

The authored partition has four disjoint groups of four. Some alternate groups are plausible: LIFE also forms LIFELINE on Crossed meanings, but assigning it there prevents the intended JACKET group. The game accepts its authored partition, without claiming every other connection is false or a mathematically unique semantic solution. Failed submissions reveal no category. Compound links may be a single word or a spaced phrase; general help explains this only when opened. Categories are revealed by requested progressive hints or successful submissions.

Dictionary spot checks used the [Oxford jacket entry](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/american_english/jacket), [Cambridge sea cucumber entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/sea-cucumber) and [Merriam-Webster elbowroom entry](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/elbowroom). Boards and arrangements were authored for this project; no publisher's puzzle or dictionary definitions were copied. Spot checks do not constitute full independent content review.

Challenging attempts use `wordplay` mode and `connections-wordplay-1` content version. Their IDs derive from immutable authored IDs. Classic retains `explore`, `starter-1` for the first two boards and `connections-2` for the other six. Replay starts a new attempt on the same board; another puzzle cycles within the chosen collection. Confirming a collection change separates the old attempt, clears selection and starts the first board of the new collection. Filed records remain unchanged. Necessary progress persists with history off; optional request/undo/timing counts do not accumulate. This adds no upload or permission path.

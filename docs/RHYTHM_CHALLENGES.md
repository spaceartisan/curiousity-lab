# Chosen six-beat sequences

The three Classic four-beat rhythms preserve their exact IDs, beat offsets, mode and content versions. Three original Challenging six-beat patterns add Two trios, Separated pairs, and Four and two. These use longer spaces between groups, but no timer, passing score, penalty or completion gate. Every full tap sequence ends as exploration regardless of spacing.

Relative-spacing feedback uses the existing algorithm and unchanged Gentle/Extra flexible thresholds. Tempo remains the player's choice; harder content does not tighten motor tolerance. Feedback is descriptive, not a validated musical proficiency or cognitive measure. A requested Hint displays actual successive offset differences, not an invented pattern. Longer help stays closed.

Confirming a challenge choice starts a distinct attempt and retains the free-piano recording. Canceling preserves progress. Another rhythm stays within the chosen collection; Try again repeats the same content. Save validation derives the four- or six-tap bound from the actual exercise, rejecting impossible solved/progress combinations. Existing four-beat saves retain their interpretation.

Tap count persists, while raw timestamps remain only in component memory. After reload or another remount with partial progress, completion explicitly reports unavailable timing feedback rather than judging the partial timing sequence. A fresh Try again permits feedback for a full sequence in one visit. No tap timestamps or match result are written to history or exports. Optional history identifies `rhythm:challenging` and `music-rhythms-2`; completion is `explored`, and filed source records remain immutable. History-off retains necessary progress without optional request/timing accumulation. There is no new data field, cloud permission or upload path.

Perceived difficulty, family preferences, actual-device audibility/accessibility and independent music-content review remain pending.

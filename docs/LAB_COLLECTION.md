## Evidence and review boundaries

New individual PHIL records were checked on 2026-10-11. Reuse statements require credit to CDC and known contributors, with the CDC website identified as the free source. Public bundle files retain the exact downloaded CDC-published low-resolution JPG bytes; no color, crop, enhancement or biological content alteration was applied. Their source derivative URLs, SHA-256 hashes and dimensions are in `public/media/provenance.json`. Original two files and IDs are preserved.

Descriptions/chips were checked against each source caption and the actual visible image. Organism names are source attribution rather than an identification inferred by the app. No cultivation conditions, sample-processing instructions, diagnosis or required scored task is introduced. Independent specialist review remains pending for all eight; this is explicit in provenance and the optional source description. PHIL 10891 was considered but omitted because the published view was less suitable for the intended morphology exploration.

The collection balances four bacterial and four fungal examples. Six new authored fictional cards remain clearly separate from the real image source. Existing workflows/slots/content versions remain unchanged; new finalized source-bound records use `lab-micrographs-2`. Notebook revisits restore actual IDs, notes and final digital view settings. All eight local slide pages validate independently and old backups stay valid.

Comparison can show any other slide, with full-source images and attribution. Its selector is temporary presentation state, not a recorded comparison answer or an association between real specimens. Selected comparison does not transfer notes, change a finalized session, or imply matched magnification. No cloud permissions or activity upload path changes.

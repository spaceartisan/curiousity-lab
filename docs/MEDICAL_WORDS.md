# Medical word play · medical-roots-1

Choose **Words → Medical**. Two authored sixteen-term boards ask for four groups covering the whole board. The first connects shared organ meanings across Latin and Greek roots; the second connects Latin position prefixes. These are English medical terms incorporating classical word parts, not a Latin translation course. No medical knowledge is inferred from an incorrect grouping.

Unsolved group names and explanations remain hidden. Requested hints use the existing three-step controls. Solved groups offer closed **Explore these roots** disclosures with explanatory prose and an external reference. Reading a solved-group explanation is not an answer hint or performance measure. There is no timer, automatic help, streak penalty or diagnosis score.

## Content references

Meanings were cross-checked on 2026-10-11 against [NLM MedlinePlus word parts](https://medlineplus.gov/appendixa.html). The Latin position prefixes and ren- are also discussed in Loredana-Daniela Ispas, _Greek and Latin Affixes in Medical Terminology in Present English Language_, pp. 63–68, [University of Craiova Annals (2013)](https://cis01.central.ucv.ro/analele_universitatii/limbi_straine_aplicate/2013/volum.pdf). Explanations are authored paraphrases, not copied reference entries.

Procedure wording follows the NCI definitions of [nephrectomy](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/nephrectomy) and [phlebotomy](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/phlebotomy): nephrectomy may remove all or part of a kidney; modern phlebotomy commonly means drawing venous blood. Definitions describe words, not instructions for procedures. Independent medical specialist and human difficulty review remain pending.
